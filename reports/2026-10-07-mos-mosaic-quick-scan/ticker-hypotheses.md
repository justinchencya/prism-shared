# Ticker hypotheses

## MOS — The Mosaic Company
- **Origin**: M1 (supply-discipline cyclical) and M2 (self-help); reports [01], [02], [03].
- **Investment hypothesis**: Over a multi-year hold, MOS earns attractive returns if potash stays firm through the Jansen ramp (2027-29) and phosphate margins recover as sulfur/ammonia normalize, while the Brazil restructuring and asset sales cut cost and fund deleveraging. Killed if (a) sulfur/ammonia stay elevated through 2027 with phosphate unable to pass costs through, (b) Jansen plus Russia/Belarus supply breaks potash discipline below roughly $250/t mine-gate, or (c) leverage rises enough to force dilution or a dividend cut.
- **Open questions**: current price, EV/EBITDA, P/B, and consensus 2026/2027 EPS and EBITDA; net debt, liquidity, leverage after the tender/new notes and the Carlsbad sale; mid-cycle EBITDA estimate versus current EV; Florida permitting and reserve life; the reconciliation of Fertilizantes EBITDA vs operating loss; outcome of the Commerce review of Morocco/Russia phosphate duties; Jansen Stage 2 capex ($6.9B vs ~$8.4B conflict between [01] and [03]); buyback status.
