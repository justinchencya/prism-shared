# MOS — peers, bear case, structural risks

**Bundle**: 03 — peers-bear-case (quick scan, survey depth, ~7 search/fetch ops)
**Parent question**: MOS (The Mosaic Company) — long-term investor scan; meta-framings M1 (supply-discipline cyclical) and M2 (fixable self-help, normalized earnings underpriced)

## Questions addressed
- Competitive position vs Nutrien, ICL, K+S, Yara, OCP, Chinese/Russian producers; cost-curve position
- Strongest bear case and key sell-side debates
- Structural risks: Florida reserves/permitting, Brazil credit, sulfur/ammonia

## Findings

### Competitive position and cost curve
- **Potash: mid-pack, not low-cost.** Mosaic Q2 2026 potash cash cost was $84/t (vs $75/t a year earlier) ([Q2 summary, World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/05082026/mosaic-releases-2q26-results/)). Nutrien guides controllable cash cost of product manufactured below $60/t ([Nutrien 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001725964/000119312526335517/d129364dex992.htm)). Caveat: the two definitions may not be like-for-like. Medium conviction on a ~$20+/t gap.
- **New potash supply is the M1 risk.** BHP Jansen is expected to be lower-cost than Nutrien's and Mosaic's top Canadian operations and to add ~5% to global supply, but Stage 2 has overrun (US$2.3B charge, cost to ~US$8.4B) ([Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/bhps-fertilizer-boss-bets-on-low-cost-potash-to-challenge-rivals/)). Belarus, Russia and Canada are about two-thirds of potash trade; Nutrien has helped lift prices by idling capacity ([Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/nutrien-steers-potash-recovery-bhp-waits-wings/)). Discipline by the incumbents is real, but Jansen is a lower-cost entrant that may not follow it.
- **Phosphate: the weakest link.** Nutrien's phosphate manufactured-product gross margin went negative (-$31/t). Ma'aden cut 2026 DAP guidance to 6.0–6.5 Mt after a 28% Q2 output decline. Mosaic's phosphate segment lost $104M operating in Q2 ([CRU/BC Insight, 2026-08-18](https://www.bcinsight.crugroup.com/2026/08/18/mosaic-maaden-and-nutrien-face-sulphur-squeeze/)). Mosaic's phosphate cash conversion cost was $129/t, with lower fixed-cost absorption from curtailments ([World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/05082026/mosaic-releases-2q26-results/)). Mosaic is a US/Brazil purchaser of sulfur and ammonia. OCP (Morocco), Ma'aden and Chinese producers have either lower-cost rock/captive acid or state backing. I did not find a sourced cost-curve percentile for MOS phosphate in this pass, so its position is unquantified. ICL, K+S, Yara: not researched within budget (unknown).
- **China**: a sulphuric-acid export ban from May 2026 and an effective Hormuz closure from late Feb 2026 are the root of the current feedstock squeeze ([CRU](https://www.bcinsight.crugroup.com/2026/08/18/mosaic-maaden-and-nutrien-face-sulphur-squeeze/)). Chinese phosphate export quotas and Russian/OCP capacity additions: not found in this pass (unknown).

### Bear case and sell-side debates
- **Bear thesis**: phosphate stripping margin is squeezed by sulfur/ammonia costs even at high product prices. Q2 2026 stripping margin averaged $422/t, which did not offset input costs. Q2 net loss was $273M (EPS -$0.86), adjusted EBITDA $407M and adjusted EPS $0.13 on $2.8B revenue ([Fertilizer Daily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260810-mosaic-q2-2026-net-loss-sulfur-crisis/); [Motley Fool transcript, 2026-08-11](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/11/mosaic-mos-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).
- **Self-help (M2) keeps slipping.** Production curtailed in May, Faustina ammonia idled, Bartow at 40%, Brazil volumes 1.5 Mt vs 2.2 Mt. FY capex was cut from $1.5B to $1.2B and operating cash flow is guided to $0.9–1.0B ([Fertilizer Daily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260810-mosaic-q2-2026-net-loss-sulfur-crisis/)). Management has missed operational targets repeatedly; I did not source the history here.
- **Sell-side**: UBS cut to Neutral (PT $27 from $33), Berenberg PT $27, JPMorgan PT $19; consensus Hold, ~$27 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/wall-street-cuts-mosaic-company-135751598.html), via search summary only — fetch 404'd, so treat as medium conviction). The core debate is timing: margin recovery pushed from 2026 to 2027, versus affordability limits on phosphate demand and closure of higher-cost South American capacity (JPMorgan view, same source).
- **Bull counter (M2 support)**: the sulfur contract for Q3 settled at $705/LT, "significantly below spot" ([World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/05082026/mosaic-releases-2q26-results/)). Potash earned $195M operating profit in Q2. Brazil restructuring should cut ~$70–80M opex and ~$20–30M capex a year ([World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/09042026/mosaic-reveals-plans-to-close-brazilian-fertilizer-assets/)).

### Structural risks
- **Sulfur/ammonia**: the most acute risk. Sulfur was $522/LT in Q2 and guided higher for Q3, against $500/t in late Q3 2025 and a ~$250M Q1 2026 EBITDA headwind ([World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/05082026/mosaic-releases-2q26-results/); Yahoo/UBS summary). It is geopolitical (Hormuz, China acid ban), so duration is unknowable. Management says "the crisis will come to an end" ([AgNavigator](https://www.agnavigator.com/Article/2026/08/05/fertilizer-crisis-will-come-to-an-end-mosaic-ceo-on-q2-earnings-call/)).
- **Brazil**: March 2026 decision to divest Araxá and idle Patrocínio (~1 Mt/yr phosphate cut), ~$442M charge, reportedly after new Brazilian dam rules ([Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/mosaic-suspends-phosphate-mines-brazil-new-rules-dams/); [World Fertilizer](https://www.worldfertilizer.com/phosphates/09042026/mosaic-reveals-plans-to-close-brazilian-fertilizer-assets/)). Q2 Fertilizantes operating loss was $41M on $1B revenue. Brazil farmer credit stress: only "soft demand" confirmed here; credit-specific data not found.
- **Florida reserves/permitting**: not resolved in this pass. The 10-Q ([Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001285785/000128578526000112/mos-20260630.htm)) is the place to check (gypstack, mine permits, Hillsborough/Manatee). Unknown.
- **Balance sheet**: net debt/leverage not sourced; check 10-Q. Unknown.

## Assessment (lens: multi-year holder)
- **M1 largely supported**: potash discipline works while Nutrien and the Russia/Belarus bloc hold, but Jansen and phosphate's state-backed low-cost producers cap the upside. Returns hinge on supply additions and on feedstock normalization.
- **M2 unproven**: the "fixable self-help" is now overtaken by exogenous sulfur/ammonia shocks. Normalized earnings may be underpriced, but the date of normalization is unknowable. Net: thesis Inconclusive, leaning Weaken near term; a Hold/accumulate-on-evidence stance fits better than a conviction Buy. Conviction low-to-medium.
