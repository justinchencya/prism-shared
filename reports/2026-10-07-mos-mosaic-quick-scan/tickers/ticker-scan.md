## MOS — The Mosaic Company

**Origin meta-framings**: M1 (supply-discipline cyclical), M2 (fixable self-help)  ·  **Origin round-1 reports**: [01], [02], [03] (reports/2026-10-07-mos-mosaic-quick-scan/individual/)

**Company snapshot**: Largest US phosphate producer plus a top-3 North American potash miner, with a Brazil distribution/mining unit (Fertilizantes). Q2-26 revenue was $2.8B and potash was the profit center ($278M adj. EBITDA of $407M total) ([02](../individual/02-mosaic-operations-financials.md)). TTM revenue is $12.2B and market cap is $6.5B, so it is a small/mid-cap cyclical (yfinance, 2026-10-07).
**Why this ticker is in this report**: It is the listed pure-play on the two nutrient markets in the question. Potash is near a cycle high and phosphate margins are squeezed by sulfur/ammonia, so M1 (supply discipline) and M2 (self-help) both bear on the name.
**Investment hypothesis**: Over a multi-year hold, MOS earns attractive returns if potash stays firm through the Jansen ramp (2027-29) and phosphate margins recover as sulfur/ammonia normalize, while the Brazil restructuring and asset sales cut cost and fund deleveraging. Killed if (a) sulfur/ammonia stay elevated through 2027 with phosphate unable to pass costs through, (b) Jansen plus Russia/Belarus supply breaks potash discipline below roughly $250/t mine-gate, or (c) leverage rises enough to force dilution or a dividend cut.

### Verdicts

**Thesis verdict:** Inconclusive
**Market verdict:** Hold
**Entry condition:** Hold what you own, and start or add in tranches once there is evidence of one of two things: (1) Q4-26/Q1-27 phosphate stripping margin turning positive as sulfur/ammonia roll over, or (2) leverage visibly falling (net debt/EBITDA toward ~2.5x) after the Q4 working-capital release. Price alone, near the ~$19.7 52-week low, would also justify a small starter position, but only with sizing that tolerates dividend-cut risk.
**Hypothesis summary:** Firm potash plus a phosphate margin recovery plus Brazil/asset-sale deleveraging produce multi-year returns, without a leverage or dilution event.

Potash strength is documented ($270-290/t mine-gate guided for Q3-26, which leaves ~$20-40/t of headroom over the $250 kill line) ([01](../individual/01-nutrient-market-cycle.md)). Whether phosphate recovers, and when, is exogenous (Hormuz, China acid ban) and unknowable. That is why the thesis is Inconclusive rather than Support. At about 7.3x EV/EBITDA on depressed trailing earnings, and with the price at the JPMorgan target and ~25% below the consensus PT, a good deal of the bear case looks priced in. Net debt of ~$5.6B against a market cap of $6.5B means the equity is a levered call on phosphate normalization, and that argues for patience rather than a full position.

**Mid-cycle framing (my estimate, low conviction, unsourced as a figure)**: EV is roughly $12.1B (market cap $6.51B plus net debt ~$5.57B, before minorities and other adjustments). The 2027E consensus EBITDA of ~$2.2B would put EV at ~5.5x. That is only a mid-cycle-ish level if potash holds near $270+ and phosphate margins are normal. Current potash prices are near a high, so a true mid-cycle potash number is lower. The offset is that phosphate is trough-ish. Net: mid-cycle EBITDA of ~$2.0-2.4B is plausible, but I could not source a CRU/Argus-based normalization. Treat as a range, not a finding. Relative to that, equity upside from re-rating to ~6.5x mid-cycle EBITDA on ~$2.2B would be material (net debt fixed, so equity is highly sensitive: each 1x turn is ~$2.2B, about a third of market cap). The same leverage works against holders if EBITDA stays near $1.7B.

- **What has to be true**:
  - Potash stays at or above ~$250/t mine-gate through Jansen Stage 1 (first production mid-CY27, ~4.2 Mtpa, ~5% of supply) ([01](../individual/01-nutrient-market-cycle.md)). Stage 2 is delayed to FY31 (BHP expects first production before 30 June 2031), which is supportive for incumbents through the window ([Argus/Mining.com via search](https://www.mining.com/bhp-takes-2-3b-hit-on-jansen-as-cost-of-potash-mine-swells-again/)). Medium conviction.
  - Sulfur/ammonia normalize in 2027. Q3 sulfur was contracted at ~$705/LT, below spot, and management says "the crisis will come to an end" ([03](../individual/03-peers-bear-case.md)). Duration depends on Hormuz and the China acid ban, so low conviction.
  - Deleveraging happens without a cut. Needs Q4 working-capital release of $300-500M, the Vale option (>$1B, timing unknown), the Araxá sale, and the ~$70-80M/yr Brazil opex savings ([02](../individual/02-mosaic-operations-financials.md); [03](../individual/03-peers-bear-case.md)). Liquidity is ample near term ($2.5B undrawn revolver, covenant at 0.65x debt/cap is not binding in the data I saw). Medium conviction on near-term solvency, low on dividend safety (FCF negative, $0.88/yr dividend is ~$280M/yr on ~317M shares, implied from the -$0.86 EPS on a -$272.8M loss).
- **What would kill it**:
  - Phosphate stripping margin stays negative into 2027 (Q2-26 $422/t stripping margin did not cover input costs; segment operating loss $104M) ([03](../individual/03-peers-bear-case.md)). Falsifier (a).
  - Potash drops toward/below $250/t mine-gate as Jansen Stage 1 ramps and Russia/Belarus sanctions soften. Mosaic's potash cash cost ($84/t) is above Nutrien's guide (<$60/t) and Jansen is lower-cost, so Mosaic is a higher-cost price-taker on the way down ([03](../individual/03-peers-bear-case.md)). Falsifier (b).
  - Dividend cut or equity raise as net debt/EBITDA stays >3x. Falsifier (c). Watch the $0.22 declaration in the Q3 print.

**Open-question resolutions (scan depth)**
- **Jansen Stage 2 capex conflict**: resolved in favor of reconciliation, not contradiction. $6.9B is the Stage 2 estimate (raised ~$2B from $4.9B) and ~$8.4B is reported as the Stage 1 cost after the US$2.3B charge ([Argus via search](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2777725-bhp-s-jansen-potash-project-faces-higher-costs); [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/bhp-takes-2-3b-hit-on-jansen-as-cost-of-potash-mine-swells-again/)). Report [03] mislabeled Stage 1 as Stage 2. BHP flagged an updated Stage 2 estimate by 30 June 2026, which I did not retrieve, so Stage 2 could be higher than $6.9B. Medium conviction (search snippets).
- **Commerce review**: Russian phosphate CVD orders were retained in expedited sunset results (June 30, 2026: 24.11% EuroChem, 14.64% PhosAgro, 16.64% others), and Commerce recommended retaining Moroccan duties, despite an eight-month suspension of the measure in June that reopened the US to OCP tonnes ([FertilizerDaily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260730-russian-phosphate-countervailing-duties-sunset-review/md/), via search summary; [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2780056-us-to-review-russia-morocco-phos-duties-in-march)). The duty protection survived but the Moroccan suspension detail is not understood; the ITC vote and final Moroccan outcome are unknown. Low-medium conviction.
- **Fertilizantes EBITDA vs operating loss**: not reconciled. The 10-Q reports $1,033.8M net sales with only $6.2M gross margin in Q2, which makes a ~$60M "EBITDA" versus a $41M operating loss plausible via D&A/other items, but this is my inference, not a sourced reconciliation ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001285785/000128578526000112/mos-20260630.htm); [02](../individual/02-mosaic-operations-financials.md); [03](../individual/03-peers-bear-case.md)).
- **Buyback**: no authorization or remaining capacity mentioned in the Q2 10-Q excerpt, and FCF is negative, so treat as inactive ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001285785/000128578526000112/mos-20260630.htm)). Medium conviction (I read ~100K of 100,185 characters).
- **Tender/new notes**: up to $1.4B of tender offers (4.05% 2027, 7.30% 2028 debentures, 5.375% 2028, 4.35% 2029) funded by a new notes issue, expired Aug 14 and results announced Aug 17, 2026 ([search of 424B5/8-Ks](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001285785/000119312526347026/d292796d424b5.htm)). It pushes out near-term maturities (liability management, not deleveraging). Final tendered amount and new-note coupon not retrieved. The 10-Q figures above are as of June 30, before the tender settled.
- **Florida permitting and reserve life**: not answered; the 10-Q excerpt had nothing on reserve status. Unknown.

- **Open / unanswered**: P/B and book value; final tender amount, new-note size and coupon, and post-tender net debt; Carlsbad sale proceeds; Florida permits and reserve life; sourced mid-cycle EBITDA (CRU/Argus), and a reconciliation of the EPS consensus ($0.76 vs yfinance $1.58) and EBITDA consensus basis; Stage 2 updated capex; the final Moroccan duty outcome and the ITC step; China phosphate export policy after August 2026; sanction-thaw risk for Belarus potash.

**Price and positioning (entry timing only)**: Price is 12% below its 50-day SMA ($23.34) and 15% below its 200-day ($24.07), RSI14 29.9, -22% over 1 month and -40% over 12 months, sitting 4% above the 52-week low (yfinance technicals, 2026-10-07).
